# HydraLab reseller agreement with Glow Lab BKK (fictional)

**Fictional document for the MarginArena demo.** HydraLab, Glow Lab BKK and this agreement are invented. It shows
how a brand's pricing clause becomes a Brand Price Rule that MarginArena enforces.

## 4. Pricing and promotion

4.1 The Reseller sets its own selling prices for HydraLab products, subject to clause 4.2.

4.2 The Reseller shall not advertise HydraLab products at a headline discount greater than twenty percent (20%) off
the recommended retail price. Shop vouchers and gifts with purchase are permitted.

4.3 Recommended retail prices are those in HydraLab's current price list. For the products Glow Lab BKK sells:

| Product | Recommended retail price |
|---|---|
| Hydra Barrier Serum 30 ml (HB-30) | ฿590 |
| Ceramide Night Cream 50 g (CN-50) | ฿690 |
| Vitamin C Bright Serum 30 ml (VC-30) | ฿590 |

## How MarginArena reads clause 4.2

- Brand Price Rule: HydraLab products only; the largest headline cut is 20% of the recommended retail price; shop
  vouchers and gifts with purchase are allowed.
- A Promo Plan that cuts a HydraLab product's headline price by more than 20% cannot be approved.
- When a Campaign Invitation asks for a deeper headline cut than the clause allows, the Verdict is Escalate, because
  the Seller must choose between the Campaign and the brand agreement.
