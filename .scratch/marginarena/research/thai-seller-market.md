# Research: how big is the Thai marketplace-Seller problem, in citable numbers?

Ticket: [11-thai-seller-market](../issues/11-thai-seller-market.md). Researched 14 Oct 2026. Fee rates, fee bases and Campaign mechanics are in [marketplace-fees.md](marketplace-fees.md) and are not repeated here.

Every claim cites a numbered source (list at the end). Source marks:

- **P**: primary. The organisation that owns the number published it: a regulator, a company's own filing, transcript or help centre, or the report's own page.
- **S**: secondary. A news outlet or law firm reporting someone else's number. Thai mainstream press is used where the original is a press conference, a paywalled report or a PDF we could not open.
- **[A]**: our own arithmetic, done on 14 Oct 2026.

Every number also carries a type:

- **Official**: a regulator or a statutory company filing.
- **Company claim**: a statement by Shopee, Lazada, TikTok Shop or Sea, not audited.
- **Estimate**: an industry research firm's model, such as Google-Temasek-Bain, Momentum Works or Cube.
- **Survey**: a questionnaire or complaint database. The sample is stated.

No page needed a login. Bangkok Post pages are behind a paid bot wall (HTTP 402) and Prachachat and Matichon returned HTTP 403, so we cite other outlets that carried the same story.

**One-line pitch framing [A].** Thai Sellers net about 2% on average [26], and a Shopee FMCG PRIME Seller pays about 28% of the price in fees. A ฿100 item with a ฿50 cost earns ฿21.11 at full price but only ฿6.67 at a 20% Campaign discount. The Seller then needs **3.2× the units just to stand still**. At 30% off, every unit loses money (see 2.4). Lazada's March 2026 Birthday Campaign lifted regional platform GMV by **just over 3.5×** compared with non-Campaign periods [19]. So the right answer differs SKU by SKU. That is the decision MarginArena makes.

**Which figure to use.**

- Use **Momentum Works (฿1.14 trillion, +52%)** for the size of the marketplace channel MarginArena serves. It counts platform GMV only, and it gives the Shopee/TikTok/Lazada split [5].
- Quote **e-Conomy SEA (US$33 B, +22%)** as the conservative cross-check [2].
- Do **not** use the ETDA ฿5–6 trillion. It includes B2B and B2G trade and is a different measure [24].
- The gap between +22% and +52% comes from different methods. Momentum Works counts platform GMV, including TikTok Shop's surge [6].

### 2.4 Why the Campaign decision is close: worked illustration [A]

Inputs:

- Shopee Non-Mall FMCG, PRIME Seller: 27.82% of the price after Seller discounts, plus ฿1.07 per order ([marketplace-fees.md](marketplace-fees.md)).
- A list price of ฿100.
- Shipping, Seller vouchers and ads are left out. All of them would make the result worse.

| Product cost | Profit at ฿100 (full price) | Profit at ฿80 (20% off) | Units needed at 20% off to match full-price profit | Profit at ฿70 (30% off) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ฿50 (50% gross margin) | ฿21.11 | ฿6.67 | **3.2×** | **−฿0.54** |
| ฿60 (40% gross margin) | ฿11.11 | **−฿3.33** | never | −฿10.54 |

This table rounds once at the end. The engine rounds each fee to the satang before adding VAT, so for the first row it
gives −฿0.55 at 30% off (`backend/tests/engine/test_unit_profit.py`), the figure used in the README and the pitch.

Lazada's March 2026 Campaign lifted regional platform GMV by just over 3.5× [19]. A 20% discount on a 50%-margin SKU needs 3.2× just to break even on profit. So whether a Campaign pays depends on the SKU and the discount. A single platform-wide multiplier cannot answer it.

## 3. How often do Sellers report margin squeeze? 2026 fee rises and the reaction

### 3.1 Seller-side evidence

- **Thai e-Commerce Association survey (THECA, Q1–Q2 2026)** [26]:
  - Sample: more than 500 Thai entrepreneurs from the association's complaint database. This is not a random sample.
  - Total fees on the three big foreign platforms: **22–40% of each order**, rising often, on some platforms every quarter.
  - **Average net profit after fees: 2.3%.**
  - Ranked complaints:
    1. High, uncapped, frequently raised fees, plus duplicate charges: **96%**.
    2. Foreign Sellers who avoid duty, VAT and Thai standards and copy Thai brands: 82%.
    3. Refund and return rules that favour buyers: 74%.
    4. Forced use of platform logistics, and inflated weight charges: 65%.
    5. "Pay to play" ad pressure, with less visibility for Sellers who don't buy ads: 58%.
  - **8,000–9,000** affected Sellers have joined a Facebook group [26].
  - On 14 May 2026 the association petitioned Parliament for Seller protection [27].
  - It made three proposals: platform oversight with fee controls; a central complaints body with a 3–6-month deadline; and "Thai First" support for Thai Sellers [26].
- **Seller arithmetic on Shopee's third rise (4 Aug 2026)** [30]:
  - Sellers estimate a **cumulative fee rise of nearly 9% since January**.
  - With other platform costs, total deductions reach **about 30%** (Seller estimates).
  - This was the third Shopee change of the year, after April and June.
  - TikTok Shop Thailand also raised some categories to 13.91% on 5 Jul 2026 [30].
- **A real order statement** [27]. A durian Seller sold about ฿2.86 m in roughly a week and paid about ฿729,300 in fees, **about 25%**.
- **Who is hurt most** [28]:
  - Resellers with no brand of their own: they "can barely keep any profit".
  - Ads are hard to avoid, because products that don't advertise lose ranking.
  - The foreign platforms "raised fees three times this year, with one month's notice".
- **Consumer side** (Bangkok Biznews, page dated 14 Oct 2026) [38]:
  - The Consumer Council warns that small shops' margins are so thin that "a cost increase of just 2–3%" can make them unviable.
  - Fee-related complaint cases rose from **10 to 22 to 68** over three periods.
  - Platform complaints of all kinds numbered about 7,886, out of 13,642 complaints received from Oct 2025 to Feb 2026.

That puts the decision MarginArena makes, whether to join, at the centre of the policy debate. **Where MarginArena is different.** The tools above do one of three things:

- They **report profit after the sale**: BigSeller, ZORT.
- They **calculate one unit's margin at a price the Seller types in**: KhumMai, GetOneCart.
- They **help the platform fill the Campaign at its price band**: Shopee Easy Nomination, Lazada recommended price.

**We found no tool, marketplace or third-party, that does all of the following before the Seller accepts a Campaign Invitation:**

1. costs the Campaign's terms (discount band, required programme fees, co-funding) against the current Marketplace Profile;
2. tests the Promo Plan against competitor Counter-moves and a demand response;
3. returns a per-SKU Verdict against the Seller's Margin Floor.

That pre-commitment step is MarginArena's gap. It is also what regulators now call the "optional cost" decision [37].

## 5. What we could not verify

1. **Lazada Thailand Seller count.** Lazada has published no Thai figure since its 2022 growth number [15]. Its DBD filing gives revenue, not Sellers.
2. **Audited Campaign share of GMV.** Only Lazada's CEO statement ("over half") exists [17]. Shopee and TikTok Shop give multiples, not shares [13][17]. Cube and Momentum Works do not publish a Campaign share [4][21].
3. **Thailand rows of the paid reports.** We read Momentum Works' Thai GMV and shares in Marketeer and Thailand Business News, not in the report itself [5][6]. The e-Conomy SEA PDF is over 10 MB and would not load, so its Thai figures come from Brand Buffet and The Story Thailand [2][3].
4. **The THECA survey method.** It is a complaint database of 500+ Sellers, not a random sample [26]. Present it as "a survey of 500+ Thai SMEs", not as a market-wide average.
5. **Prachachat and Bangkok Post.** We could not open their pages (HTTP 403/402). The same events are cited from other outlets above.
