# 11 How big is the Thai marketplace-seller problem, in citable numbers?

Type: research
Status: resolved
Blocked by: none

## Question

We need citable, primary-source numbers for the pitch's Business Value:
- How many active sellers are there on Shopee Thailand and Lazada Thailand?
- What is Thailand's e-commerce or marketplace GMV, and what share of it comes from mega Campaigns like 11.11?
- How often do sellers report margin squeeze from fees and Campaign discounts? Include news about the 2026 fee hikes and seller complaints.
- What do existing pricing and promotion tools for SEA sellers offer, so we can show where MarginArena is different?

Findings: `.scratch/marginarena/research/thai-seller-market.md`

## Answer

Full findings: [research/thai-seller-market.md](../research/thai-seller-market.md) (49 dated sources)

### Pitch numbers

- **Market:** Thai platform e-commerce GMV was ฿1.14T (US$35.5B) in 2025, up 52%. Shopee 50%, TikTok Shop 32%, Lazada 18% (Momentum Works, via press).
- **Sellers:** about 1.2M Thai small Sellers have Shopee shops, and about 170k joined Shopee 9.9 in 2026.
- **Campaigns:** Lazada's CEO says over half of platform sales come from mega Campaigns.
- **Margin squeeze:** a Thai e-Commerce Association survey of 500+ SMEs found fees take 22–40% of each order and average net profit is 2.3%. 96% rank fees as their top complaint. The sample is not random.
- **Platform revenue:** Shopee Thailand's revenue grew 77% to ฿88.4bn in 2025, faster than the market.
- **Regulators:** ETDA published fee-transparency guidelines, the Trade Competition Commission (TCC) issued an e-commerce guideline, and on 14 Oct 2026 the minister set up a Platform Fee Subcommittee.

### Worked example

A ฿100 item costing ฿50 earns ฿21.11 at full price. At 20% off it earns ฿6.67, so the Seller needs 3.2× the units to earn the same. At 30% off every unit loses money.

### Gap

BigSeller, ZORT, Ginee, KhumMai, GetOneCart, aCommerce, Shopee Easy Nomination and Lazada's tools all either report profit after the sale, compute one unit's margin at a price the Seller types in, or help the platform fill its Campaign. **None costs a Campaign Invitation before the Seller commits.**
